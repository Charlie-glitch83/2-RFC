## Unsupported Module M claims

Module M does not establish:

- empirical correctness of generated masses, widths, transition amplitudes, or rates;
- observed stellar, explosive, merger, solar, galactic, or intergalactic abundance agreement;
- a unique fully instantiated numerical cosmic chemical history;
- a planetary, prebiotic, biological, habitability, neural, cognitive, or consciousness theorem;
- Module N manifested-universe assembly;
- Module O freeze, Module P validation, or Module Q terminal closure.
