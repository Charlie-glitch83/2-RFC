# Claim and Evidence Standard

## Bold final claim sought

The project is designed to earn the following claim:

> Within the stated axioms and realization conditions, the completed triadic kernel is sufficient to generate every admitted physical state in the constructed universe; the Big Implosion is its unique first physical event, the Big Rip is its terminal physical event, and the resulting frozen predictions survive preregistered empirical tests without fitting or target leakage.

When all premises and gates are actually proved, this claim should be stated directly. It should not be diluted with generic language such as “merely a framework.”

## Evidence classes never to conflate

1. Axiom or definition.
2. Formal theorem under named assumptions.
3. Internal symbolic/numerical certificate.
4. Physical simulation with dimensional laws and convergence.
5. Frozen prediction.
6. Preregistered public-data comparison.
7. Independent reproduction.

An item can be profound and still be correctly typed. Precision strengthens the eventual claim; it does not weaken it.
