## Explicit Module E boundaries

Module E does not prove:

- measured nuclear masses, bindings, spins, lifetimes, cross sections, or rate precision;
- public BBN-code equivalence;
- observed primordial abundance agreement;
- a unique SI calibration;
- continuum infinite-volume nuclear field theory;
- stellar, explosive, or late-time nucleosynthesis;
- post-nuclear plasma persistence;
- recombination, CMB formation, structure formation, or empirical confirmation.

Earlier W2-R/W3/W4 results remain structural and historical assets. Their proxy scores and public comparisons are not promoted into the generated Module E reaction network.

These checks verify mathematics and implementation examples; they do not establish empirical nuclear truth.

## Evidence classes never to conflate

1. definition or axiom;
2. theorem under named assumptions;
3. abstract construction theorem;
4. symbolic or numerical verification;
5. physical law and converged simulation;
6. frozen prediction;
7. preregistered public-data comparison;
8. independent reproduction.

A code pass is not a theorem. An internally generated resemblance is not empirical confirmation. No average, global score, or favorable example may hide a failed mandatory condition.
