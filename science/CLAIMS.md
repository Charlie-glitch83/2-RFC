## Explicit Module C boundaries

Module C does not prove:

- empirical identification of its internally generated states with nature;
- measured masses, couplings, mixings, widths, lifetimes, or abundances;
- a unique SI calibration;
- continuum Lorentzian renormalized quantum field theory;
- complete loop or running-coupling precision;
- lattice-QCD precision;
- a thermal history;
- surviving baryon asymmetry;
- public empirical agreement.

The familiar `U(1) x SU(2) x SU(3)` structure and rational charges arise here as internal finite-relational outputs. Their resemblance to a known external theory is not used as proof or as a generative input.

## Evidence classes never to conflate

1. definition or axiom;
2. theorem under named assumptions;
3. abstract construction theorem;
4. symbolic or numerical verification;
5. physical law and converged simulation;
6. frozen prediction;
7. preregistered public-data comparison;
8. independent reproduction.

A code pass is not a theorem. An internal generated resemblance is not empirical confirmation. No average, global score, or favorable example may hide a failed mandatory condition.
