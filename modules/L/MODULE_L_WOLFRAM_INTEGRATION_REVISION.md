## Authorized work

Use Wolfram for baryonic, stellar, and feedback physics:

- conservative hydro/MHD, radiation, cosmic-ray, and thermochemistry equations;
- dimensional and tensor consistency;
- shocks, turbulence, vorticity, mixing, instability, and dispersion relations;
- cooling/heating and chemical equilibrium or nonequilibrium systems;
- cloud, disc, core, fragmentation, and witnessed star-birth conditions;
- protostellar and stellar-structure boundary-value problems;
- binaries, mass transfer, compact remnants, accretion, jets, feedback, and ionization;
- source-term conservation and K–L–M return closure;
- stiff Jacobians, bifurcations, sensitivities, covariance, and replay-locality tests.

## Required methods

Use symbolic conservation reduction, PDE/ODE limiting cases, arbitrary-precision stellar and chemical solves, instability analysis, manufactured solutions, and independent hydro/MHD or stellar formulations.

## Forbidden use

Wolfram may not insert a measured IMF, empirical star-formation law, tuned feedback efficiency, public galaxy relation, or observational calibration into the generative Module L physics.

## Completion addition

Every star-birth witness, stellar trajectory, feedback channel, compact-object state, and return packet requires a Wolfram-backed derivation or verification and an independent conservation/formulation check.
