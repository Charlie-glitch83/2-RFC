# Module M — Wolfram Integration Revision

Binding revision to `MODULE_M_DETAILED_SCIENTIFIC_REPAIR_PLAN.md`; governed by `architecture/2RFC_WOLFRAM_INTEGRATION_RULES.md`.

## Authorized work

Use Wolfram for stellar and explosive nucleosynthesis, enrichment, and composition feedback:

- typed isotope and nuclear-state registries;
- reaction and decay hypergraphs, stoichiometry, conserved quantities, and cycles;
- forward/reverse rates, equilibrium, partition functions, and detailed balance;
- adaptive hydrostatic, mixing-burning, and explosive networks;
- winds, eruptions, mass transfer, common-envelope, merger, collapse, and compact-merger source trajectories;
- total, net, retained, fallback, escaped, radioactive, and dust-bearing yields;
- daughter chains, delayed energy, neutrino losses, and energy closure;
- isotope-conserving enrichment transport and source attribution;
- composition-dependent cooling, opacity, heating, dust, and molecular state;
- lineage-derived delay-time distributions, covariance, and K/L return sensitivities.

## Required methods

Use exact stoichiometry, sparse stiff integration, symbolic rate identities, adaptive-network convergence, decay-chain matrix methods, and independent conservation and reduced/full-network checks.

## Forbidden use

Published yield tables, empirical delay-time distributions, solar abundance patterns, or target chemical histories may be benchmarks only unless explicitly authorized as interface inputs; they may not generate the RFC yields.

## Completion addition

Every isotope yield, decay history, transport parcel, source attribution, and composition-feedback return requires Wolfram-backed conservation, energy, uncertainty, and network-convergence evidence.
