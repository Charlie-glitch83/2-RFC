## External-only material

Public catalogues, observations, instrument characterizations, standard databases and conventional models enter only here under declared roles: calibration, validation, prediction test or benchmark. They do not become upstream RFC sources.

## New derivations required

- complete truth-to-observation operators;
- instrument, survey, selection, noise, foreground and catalogue models;
- total and cross-probe covariance;
- identifiability and information-loss classification;
- preregistered statistics, residuals and falsifiers;
- same-parent multi-probe consistency;
- evidence-matched decision and independent replication.

## Forbidden promotion

Do not use public results to choose the parent, realization, observer, branch, normalization, law or constant. Statistical agreement is not automatically validation; validation is not automatically predictive confirmation. Any mandatory normalized component below `0.95` remains failure regardless of aggregate likelihood.
