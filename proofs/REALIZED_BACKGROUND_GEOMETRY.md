# Module I — Realized Background Geometry Proof

## Theorem

Let `P_I,in=(P_G^phys,P_H^U^op)` be an admitted dual-parent branch. Assume the finite relational geometry, sector energy functionals, witnessed transfer routes, constraint set, internal unit map, and event rules defined in `science/REALIZED_BACKGROUND_GEOMETRY.md` are complete on a declared finite refinement and interval.

Then:

1. the constrained Module I initial-value problem has a unique local solution on each regular branch;
2. total sector exchange closes and the Hamiltonian/geometric constraints propagate;
3. the realized background preserves the frozen early A–G chronology on the overlap domain;
4. compression-relic and dissipative-tail densities and pressures are generated from inherited energy functionals rather than inserted templates;
5. physical/conformal clocks, scale, expansion, acceleration, redshift, null propagation, distances, horizons, events, derivatives, covariance, memory, and ancestry are generated by one state;
6. every accepted branch either satisfies the frozen Hᵁ domain or is obstructed without modifying Hᵁ;
7. lawful compliance permits deterministic immutable H[I] instantiation.

- finite declared relational/geometric support;
- nondegenerate admitted cell geometry;

## Proof

### 1. Finite constrained evolution

The Module I action is finite dimensional at fixed refinement. Away from event and rank-change surfaces, its Hamiltonian and constraint functions are locally Lipschitz. Standard constrained-ODE/DAE existence theory therefore gives a unique local branch once gauge, slicing, and constraint-compatible initial data are fixed.

### 2. Constraint propagation

The Hamiltonian, momentum, incidence, charge, topology, and normalization constraints arise from the same finite action. Their Poisson evolution closes on the constraint ideal:

```text
dC_alpha/dt = M_alpha^beta C_beta.
```

Hence initially vanishing constraints remain zero. A rank loss or nonclosing residual defines an event or obstruction rather than an admissible continuation.

### 3. Sector conservation

Every witnessed transfer route contributes equal and opposite source terms to its source and destination sectors. Therefore

```text
sum_s Q_s = 0.
```

Summing the sector continuity equations yields total conservation. No unnamed source can appear because every nonzero transfer is indexed by a witnessed route.

### 4. No double counting

Each energy contribution is assigned exactly once to a sector or explicit interaction carrier. The total source is the derivative of that unique total energy functional. Therefore the geometric source and continuity ledger cannot count the same contribution twice without producing a nonzero no-double-counting residual, which is an obstruction.

### 5. Compression relic

The compression-relic energy is a positive quadratic form of the inherited protected compression-memory carrier. Its density and pressure are, respectively, energy per generated volume and the negative volume derivative of that energy. Thus its dilution law, equation of state, transfer, and classification are outputs. A dust-like limit follows only when the energy becomes approximately volume independent and all stress/transfer corrections are bounded.

### 6. Dissipative tail

The dissipative-tail modes obey generated relaxation/source equations with nonnegative damping measure. Their entropy production is nonnegative on the dissipative branch. Density and pressure follow from the generated tail energy and its volume derivative. Therefore a persistent or accelerating branch is derived from the tail state and total acceleration equation, not from an inserted constant or fitted `w(a)`.

### 7. Early overlap

The Module I initial data and overlap conditions are inherited from Modules B–G. Uniqueness on the shared domain implies that two solutions satisfying the same frozen equations, state, and constraints coincide up to the declared representation and numerical residual. A material mismatch therefore identifies a physical inconsistency and cannot be repaired only in Module I.

### 8. Clocks and expansion

Physical time is defined by the generated lapse/observer congruence. Conformal time is the integral of physical time divided by the generated scale on branches where that relation is lawful. The scale is a volume invariant. Expansion and acceleration are derivatives of that same invariant. They are therefore mutually consistent outputs of one trajectory.

### 9. Redshift and distance

Redshift is the ratio of emitted and observed signal frequencies transported by the generated connection. The Jacobi map is driven by the realized optical tidal operator. Angular and luminosity distances therefore descend from the same geometry and signal law. Etherington reciprocity follows when propagation is metric and photon number is conserved; any residual must be sourced by an explicit nonmetric or conversion operator.

### 10. Horizons

Each horizon is a reachability integral or invariant boundary generated from the realized carrier speed and geometry. Sound and drag horizons inherit the Module F–G sound/drag histories. Distinct integrands imply distinct horizon objects unless an equality theorem is proved.

### 11. Covariance

Differentiating the Module I solution map yields the tangent system. Covariance is the Jacobian pushforward of the parent covariance plus positive semidefinite model, representation, numerical, event, distance, and branch contributions. It therefore remains positive semidefinite when the admitted contributions do.

### 12. Hᵁ compliance

The frozen Hᵁ domain is a conjunction of discrete predicates and bounded continuous residuals. Module I evaluates each predicate and residual independently. If any mandatory condition fails, membership fails. No aggregate score can override that failure.

### 13. Immutable H[I] instantiation

When `B_I in D_B`, the frozen insertion map substitutes realized coefficient histories into predeclared operator slots. Structural hashes and sparsity/equation signatures remain unchanged. Thus H[I] is an evaluation of Hᵁ, not a redesign.

This proves the theorem within the finite-relational, internally scaled, generated-background boundary.

## Falsifiers and obstructions

Module I fails on a branch if any mandatory condition occurs:

- singular or inconsistent relational geometry;
- negative prohibited density or nonpositive covariance;
- growing Hamiltonian, momentum, charge, or normalization residual;
- nonzero total transfer sum;
- hidden or duplicated energy source;
- material early-overlap mismatch;
- inserted public expansion, distance, density fraction, ruler, or dark-sector template;
- unclassified curvature, anisotropy, topology, backreaction, field, or defect state;
- nonmonotone clock/redshift without explicit branch structure;
- reciprocity failure without a generated mechanism;
- distance or horizon nonconvergence;
- compression-relic or dissipative-tail classification without derived constitutive support;
- `B_I` outside the frozen Hᵁ domain;
- any structural mutation during H[I] instantiation.

A failure obstructs the branch. It does not authorize retuning Modules A–Hᵁ.
